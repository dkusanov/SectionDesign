About SectionLab 2D

SectionLab 2D

Version: 1.0.0 (beta)

Danilo S. Kusanovic

Assistant Professor of Teaching

Civil and Environmental Engineering

University of California Davis

A reinforced-concrete cross-section analysis tool built on strain compatibility and equilibrium. Features include interactive section and reinforcement editing, uniaxial P-Mx / P-My interaction diagrams, biaxial Mx-My contours, the 3D P-Mx-My capacity surface, nonlinear fiber moment-curvature response, factored demand-point checks, dynamic unit conversion, and a printable engineering report.

Educational and investigation use only. All results must be independently verified by a licensed engineer before design or construction use.
